How it works

  1. Fill out the 2-minute form.
  2. I call you within one business day to learn the work, schedule and pay you want.
  3. When a job fits, I tell you the employer and the details. You decide yes or no.
  4. You interview with the employer directly. I help you prepare and I handle the offer conversation.

What I will never ask you for

  • Money of any kind. No fees, ever, and nothing comes out of your paycheck.
  • Your Social Security number, bank or card details, or photos of your ID. Those go to the employer after a real offer, never to me.
  • To deposit a check, buy gift cards or send money anywhere.
  • To move our conversation to WhatsApp, Telegram or another chat app.
  • What you earn now or earned before. New York law bars that question.

Questions people ask

Why is this free for me?

Employers pay recruiters to find skilled people they can't find on their own. You never pay anything, and New York law doesn't let an employer take a recruiting cost out of your pay.

I'm not sure I want to leave my job.

That's fine. A lot of people just want to know what they're worth. We talk, you hear what's out there, and you decide. No obligation.

Do I need a resume?

No. Tell me about your work on the form or on the phone and I'll put it together.

What kinds of jobs?

HVAC, plumbing, electrical, CDL-A driving, maintenance, welding, auto and diesel, IT help desk and LPN roles around Binghamton and Broome County.

Is this legit?

It's a new local business, so there isn't much about it online yet. You never pay, I never ask for financial or ID information, and I name the employer before any interview so you can confirm the job yourself.

Are you part of the City of Binghamton?

No. Parlor City is Binghamton's nickname. This is a private business, not affiliated with the city or any government agency.

Is a job guaranteed?

No. I connect you with employers who are hiring. The employer makes the hiring decision.
